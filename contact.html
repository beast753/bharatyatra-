Quick help

Answers before you wait

How do I cancel a booking?

Open My Bookings, select your ticket, and choose “Cancel & request refund.” Eligibility depends on the operator’s cancellation window.

Where can I find my PNR?

Your PNR appears on the confirmation ticket and in My Bookings. Include it in your message for faster support.

When will I receive my refund?

Once the operator confirms the cancellation, refunds are generally processed to the original payment method.
