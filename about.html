How booking works on BharatYatra

1. Search

Pick a from/to city and date on the ticket-shaped search form on the homepage.

2. Compare

Browse bus results with operator, timing, fare and rating, and sort them by what matters to you.

3. Book

Pick real seats on a bus-shaped seat map, fill in passenger details, and get an instant PNR confirmation.
